Our target

We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA, including keyboard access, readable contrast, meaningful headings and usable forms. This is a target, not a claim of a completed independent audit or full conformity. On 22 October 2026 we performed automated axe-core checks and browser-based keyboard, contrast, consent and mobile-size checks on the local preview at 1440 and 390 pixels. The checks do not establish full conformity or replace testing with assistive technology.

Known limitations

The interactive globe and canvas charts are primarily visual and do not expose all geographic or chart detail to screen readers. The News page and article lists provide text access to headlines and sources. Keyboard access, focus visibility, scrollable tables, reduced motion and key control sizes have been improved. Canvas content still needs fuller text alternatives. Full browser zoom, screen-reader usability, dense chart-pin spacing, authenticated account flows and third-party sign-in need further human verification. Report a barrier

Contact feedback@investedia.com [OWNER: confirm mailbox forwarding and monitoring] with the page URL, your device/browser and what you were trying to do. Include your assistive technology if relevant, without sending sensitive data. We will assess the issue and seek a reasonable accessible alternative. We do not promise a verified response deadline or claim that all identified barriers are already resolved.
