Information, not advice

Investedia provides general information and education only. Nothing here is personalised investment, tax or legal advice, an offer, or a recommendation to buy, sell, hold, short or go long on an asset. We do not assess your finances, objectives, experience or risk tolerance.

You can lose money

Investments can fall in value and you may lose some or all of your money. Past performance and illustrative calculations do not predict future results. Currency movements, costs and taxes can change outcomes. Cryptoassets are particularly volatile and high risk; protections vary and may be limited.

What scenarios mean

“Scenarios to watch” describe possible developments and uncertainty. They are not forecasts, trading signals or suggested positions. A company’s appearance in a article, watchlist or exposure map is not an endorsement. An AI-generated explanation can be incomplete or wrong.

Check before relying

Prices may be delayed, incomplete or stale. Different sources and timestamps can disagree. We do not guarantee accuracy, completeness, uninterrupted service or investment outcomes. Read original sources, do your own research and consider an appropriately licensed adviser for your circumstances.
