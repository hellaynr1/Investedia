# Finviz

Source: https://finviz.com (fetched 2026-10-17)

## Observed
- Positioning: stock screener + heat maps + news + insider trading + futures/forex/crypto quotes.
- Free tier: quotes delayed 1 min (futures/options 15 min), ads shown; screener, maps, news, calendars visible.
- Paid "Elite": from $24.96/mo per homepage; real-time data, advanced charting, alerts, no ads, exports/API.
- CTAs: "Try Elite for Free" (top banner), "Start Your Free Elite Trial" (bottom), "Sign up free", "Watch the Guided Tour", CSV export.
- Homepage leads with live market-breadth stats and a headline summary.
- Note: third-party comparison sites list other prices ($39.50/mo); homepage figure is more reliable.

## Interpretation
- Funnel is free tool → habit → ad removal/real-time upsell. Product, not content, drives acquisition.
- Investedia's overlap: heat maps (globe/markets), screener, calendar. Differentiators: beginner education, POV calculator, plain-English framing, global coverage.

## Test ideas
- Add a "guided tour" for first-time visitors on index.html.
- Lead the homepage with a one-line market-breadth summary (data already in assets/quotes.js).

## Avoid
- Competing on real-time data depth; Investedia's data is free-source and delayed.
