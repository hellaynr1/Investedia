# Logo check: Investedia vs Investing.com (2026-10-24)

Observed
- Investedia (assets/logo.png, favicon.svg): black square, white rounded sans wordmark "Investedia" with an orange dot on the "i", orange (#FF7A1A) line chart over dark grey area.
- Investing.com favicon (via Google favicon service, 128px): dark rounded square, bold white "Inv" with an orange accent block on the "v". Full wordmark NOT viewed: investing.com returns 403 to automated fetch.

Interpretation (not legal advice)
- Overlap: dark background + white sans-serif + orange accent, and both names start "Invest". That is a similar mood in the same category, which could cause casual confusion at small sizes.
- Difference: Investedia's line-chart motif and full-word wordmark are distinct from "Inv" lettering.
- Orange/black is common in finance (Investopedia also uses orange per one search result), so the palette alone is weak ground for confusion.

Next steps
- View investing.com's full logo in a real browser or brand page and compare side by side.
- Consider shifting the accent hue or adding a distinctive shape. Trademark clearance needs a lawyer or a USPTO/EUIPO search.
