Key takeaways

    Big Tech will spend around $600 billion on data centers next year, more than the entire US oil and gas industry invests in a year. The question for investors is no longer whether the money gets spent, but who keeps it.

    Following the dollar

    We split each dollar of AI data-center spending into five buckets: chips, networking, power and cooling, buildings, and everything else. Chips still take the largest share, but power is the fastest-growing slice.
