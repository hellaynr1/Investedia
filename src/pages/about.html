Our story

Investedia started in 2024 with a simple frustration: investing felt harder than it needed to be. Prices in one app, news on another site, explanations buried in jargon, and tools scattered everywhere.

So our team set out to put it all in one place. Clear charts for every market. Tools that answer real questions. Explanations anyone can follow. A place where you can explore the markets, understand the trends and find the insights that matter, without the noise.

Our mission is to make investing simple and accessible for everyone, wherever they start from.

What we stand for

A tool, not a promise.

Investedia gives you clarity. What you do with it is always your decision.

  • Clarity over noise

    Simple charts and plain language. No jargon for the sake of it.

Investedia app

Put Investedia on your home screen.

Our app installs straight from the website. It's free, takes a few seconds, and opens full screen like any other app. No App Store needed.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open investedia.com in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open investedia.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
