Selection and context

We aim to cover business, markets, companies, the economy, technology, crypto, commodities and economically relevant geopolitics. Automated source collection, Gemini selection and field validation organise the saved feed. Importance labels reflect editorial relevance, not a recommendation or prediction of a price move.

Independence and funding

No advertising, affiliate links or paid article placement is currently declared for this site. Future paid relationships must be clearly labelled and separated from independent editorial judgment. We will not describe promotional material as independent reporting. See Advertising & affiliates.

Verification and limits

Sources, timestamps and “Illustration” labels help readers assess content. article are automated with validation; there is no promise of routine human review of every summary. Original publishers can update their articles after our feed is saved. Claims and context can be wrong, and readers should check important information against primary sources.

Request a correction

Email info@investedia.com [OWNER: confirm mailbox forwarding and monitoring] with the article URL, the specific error and reliable supporting evidence. We assess material accuracy issues and may correct, clarify, remove or leave content with an explanation. Material corrections handled through this process should be recorded in the corrections log, with the date and nature of the change. Routine feed refreshes are not individually logged.
