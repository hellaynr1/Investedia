How to read this

  • Confirmed means the asset moved at least 0.3% the way the article implied. Went the other way means it moved at least 0.3% against it. Smaller moves are no clear move.
  • The move is measured from the first price recorded after the article to the latest recorded price, then fixed at 24 hours after the article. Stocks are delayed 15–30 minutes.
  • Pending means no price has been recorded after the article yet, for example while the market is closed.

Article directions are written with the help of AI from each source's reporting. Past results do not predict future ones. Investedia is media, not advice. See the overall track record.
