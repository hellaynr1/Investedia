Questions

Does Pro make the predictions more accurate?

No. Every user sees the same Market Views, and their full record is public on the Track Record page. Pro adds alerts, research depth and tools. Nothing here is investment advice.

What happens after the free trial?

You are charged €11.99 per month unless you cancel before the trial ends. We email you 24 hours before.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from your account page in one click. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Do you show VAT?

Prices include VAT for consumers in the EU. Business customers can add a VAT number at checkout.
