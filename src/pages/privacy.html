Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026. Plain English, no surprises.

Who we are

Investedia (investedia.com) is a free website with market data, news, tools and lessons about investing. Investedia is responsible for the personal data described here. Questions or requests: info@investedia.com.

What we collect

  • Account details, if you create an account: your email address, the name you give us, and a password (stored only as a secure hash, never readable by us). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Your email choice: whether you agreed to receive the daily brief and market news.
  • Your settings, such as your watchlist, alerts and display preferences.
  • Technical data needed to run the site, such as your IP address and browser type, which our hosting and login providers process when you visit.

You can use Investedia as a guest without an account. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell your personal data.

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in (to provide the service you asked for).
  • To send the emails needed for your account, such as confirming your email address or resetting your password.
  • To send the daily brief and market news, only if you ticked the box (your consent). You can unsubscribe at any time from any email or your profile.
  • To keep the site safe and working.

Who helps us

We use a small number of trusted providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: account login and storage.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Our email provider: to deliver account emails and, if you agreed, the newsletter.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting of the website.
  • Market data and font services (such as Coinbase for live prices and Google Fonts): your browser connects to them directly to load prices and fonts.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Stored on your device

Investedia saves some things in your browser's local storage, such as your theme, whether you have seen a pop-up, your watchlist and your login session. This is needed for the site to work and is not used to track you across other websites.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as you have an account. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, unless we must keep something by law. If you unsubscribe from emails, we stop sending them straight away.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw your consent to emails at any time. Write to info@investedia.com and we will reply within 30 days. If you live in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Investedia accounts are not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top, and tell account holders by email about important changes.

Investedia provides information and education only. Nothing on this site is investment advice. See our terms.
