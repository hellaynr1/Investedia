Data we actually use

For accounts, Supabase processes your email, password authentication information, user ID and authentication/session metadata. Your password is sent to Supabase for authentication; the site does not store a readable password. Google sign-in can provide your name, email, profile picture and provider identifier. Your account can hold a name, newsletter choice and watchlist. Country, topic and article follows are stored on the device in this branch.

Device storage can contain guest lists, country/topic follows, learning progress, display choices and popup-dismissal flags. Our storage inventory identifies the keys. Messages you send us include the contact details and information you provide.

Hosting, authentication and email services process technical logs such as IP addresses, request times, browser information and delivery/security events. We do not claim these logs are anonymous.

How long data is kept

Account and list data is needed while an account is active. Device keys without an expiry remain until cleared, withdrawn where applicable, or removed by your browser; closing a tab does not clear local storage. Consent expires after 12 months or a policy-version change. Session-only flags end with the browser session.

The current code does not enforce a scheduled deletion period for database events, account backups or provider logs. We therefore do not promise deletion within a fixed number of days. Your rights and choices

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability where applicable, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw optional consent through Cookie settings without affecting earlier lawful processing. Write to info@investedia.com [OWNER: confirm mailbox forwarding and monitoring], describing your request and the relevant account; we may ask for proportionate identity verification.

We aim to handle GDPR requests within one month, subject to the Regulation’s permitted extensions and exceptions. We will explain any extension or refusal and the available remedies. You can complain to Belgium’s Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données via its official complaints page, or another competent supervisory authority.

Children, security and changes

Accounts are intended for ages 16 and over; this is a service policy, not a claim about Belgium’s statutory age of digital consent. Contact us if a child’s data needs attention. We use HTTPS and provider authentication/access controls, but no system is completely secure.
