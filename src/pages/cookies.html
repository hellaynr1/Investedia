Your choices

Necessary storage supports consent, login and features you request. Preferences, external media/services and existing first-party usage statistics are optional and off until chosen. Accept all and Accept essential are offered at the same level. Accept essential keeps all optional categories off; Accept all enables them together. The site remains readable when optional items are rejected.

This inventory comes from the site’s localStorage/sessionStorage calls and offline-cache code. Browser local storage has no automatic expiry unless the code provides one. It is different from a browser cookie. Authentication providers may also use their own security/session mechanisms when you sign in.

Optional third-party requests

Third-party images & services allows Brandfetch logos, Google-hosted fonts and direct Coinbase rate requests. Without it, the site uses local logo/initial fallbacks, system fonts and saved market data. Locally saved Pixabay photographs still work. Following an external link or choosing Google sign-in is a separate user-requested action.

The existing Supabase usage-events feature is also optional. It sends limited page and interaction information only with Analytics consent; there are no advertising trackers added by this change.

Changing or withdrawing consent

Your inv-consent choice records the policy version, date and categories. We ask again after 12 months or a version change. Cookie settings in the footer reopens the two choices and shows your current selection. Withdrawing Preferences clears the optional preference keys listed above. Previously sent requests cannot be recalled; future optional requests stop. If storage is blocked, a choice lasts for the current page and the card may reappear on your next visit.
