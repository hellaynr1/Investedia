Our target

We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA, including keyboard access, readable contrast, meaningful headings and usable forms. This is a target, not a claim of a completed independent audit or full conformity.

Known limitations

The interactive globe and canvas charts are primarily visual and do not expose all geographic or chart detail to screen readers. The News page and story lists provide text access to headlines and sources. Third-party sign-in and linked sites may have limitations outside our control. Report a barrier

Contact info@investedia.com [OWNER: confirm mailbox forwarding and monitoring] with the page URL, your device/browser and what you were trying to do. Include your assistive technology if relevant, without sending sensitive data. We will assess the issue and seek a reasonable accessible alternative. We do not promise a verified response deadline or claim that all identified barriers are already resolved.
