Copyright and trademarks

Investedia claims rights only in material it owns. Publishers, photographers, data providers and trademark owners retain their rights. Third-party names and logos identify sources or entities; their appearance is not an endorsement. Linking does not transfer copyright.

Send a clear notice

For copyright, trademark or other allegedly illegal content, write to copyright@investedia.com [OWNER: confirm mailbox forwarding and monitoring], with “Content notice” in the subject. Include the exact URL, the material at issue, your contact details, the right or legal concern involved, supporting evidence, and a good-faith explanation of why it is unlawful or used without permission. If acting for someone else, explain your authority.

Do not send passwords or unnecessarily sensitive information. A reasoned notice helps us locate and assess the content; you do not need to use a particular legal phrase.

Assessment and response

We aim to acknowledge notices, assess them fairly and act without undue delay where appropriate. We may seek clarification, restrict disputed content, correct attribution or remove it, and explain our decision where lawful. Serious safety or illegality concerns may need urgent handling. No fixed response time or continuous monitoring is promised.

Challenge or counter-notice

If you believe a restriction was mistaken, reply with the notice reference, your connection to the material and evidence supporting lawful use. We will reassess and explain the outcome where appropriate. These procedures take account of EU notice-and-action principles, including the Digital Services Act where applicable; they do not assert that Investedia is a hosting intermediary or remove anyone’s right to seek legal remedies.
