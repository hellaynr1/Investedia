What AI assists with

Google Gemini helps select financially relevant news, group related headlines and draft summaries and context. Automated validation checks fields, categories, locations and output limits before a saved feed is published. This is automated processing with validation, not a claim that a human approves every article.

What the model sees

The news workflow supplies public source headlines and short descriptions and reference information used to organise the news. It does not send account emails, private watchlists or visitor messages to Gemini. Summaries link to their original sources rather than reproducing full articles.

Limits and transparency

Models can misread context, omit important details or produce errors. Validation cannot establish that every statement is true. AI transparency here follows the spirit of informing readers, including the objectives of EU AI Act article 50; this is not a claim of certified compliance or a substitute for checking which legal obligations apply.

The galaxy in the homepage background is an AI-generated decorative image, not a photograph of a real galaxy.
