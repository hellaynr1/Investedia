"Since the news came out": three ways to show it

Goal you described: anyone can see, live, how an asset has moved since the article was published, so Investedia becomes a tool people use to act. Each option answers three things: what happened (article), when (marker on the chart), and what the market did next. The chart curves in these pictures are illustrative; the article, times and today's moves (Brent −6.16%, Meta −0.96%) are real. Real tracking needs one new robot that records the price of each affected asset when the article is published and every 15 minutes after (stocks are delayed 15–30 min; crypto can tick live). Not investment advice.

Extends the "Why it moved" card you showed me. The big number becomes "since the article", with milestone chips (+15 min, +1 h, +3 h, now) and a "Live" badge. Used on the homepage, in the globe story panel and on news article pages.
