Globe on phone: three options

Problems in the current phone globe: three stacked control rows (search, topic chips, region chips) take a third of the screen before you see the globe; chips are cut off at the edge; the time-window bar floats mid-screen; the news sheet is closed to a title with a thin handle; panel tabs are 34 px tall; text is 12–13 px grey. All three options below use tap targets of 44 px or more, text of 14 px or more and lighter grey for contrast. Screens use today's real stories and real asset moves.
